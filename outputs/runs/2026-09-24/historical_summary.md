# Historical Thursday / Friday evidence

Decision span: 2025-09-25 to 2026-09-24. Calendar Thursdays considered: 53; successfully scanned: 50. Completed next-Friday sessions: 46; Friday holidays skipped: 3; pending Friday dates: 1.

Median eligible stock universe: 499.5. Qualified name-events: 2718; PM-visible: 500; unscorable qualifiers: 6; negative absolute impulse qualifiers: 219.

The all-qualified population includes unscorable qualifiers in unconditional outcomes; score buckets exclude them. PM-top10 evaluates only each Thursday's displayed names. Rates use completed, valid Friday observations only. All returns below are decimal fractions, not percent units.

## Observed findings

all_qualified: higher leans closed above the range in 309/1221 cases (25.3%), versus 99/1224 (8.1%) for lower leans. Mean Friday excess returns were 0.002% and 0.052%, respectively.

pm_top10: higher leans closed above the range in 70/273 cases (25.6%), versus 16/187 (8.6%) for lower leans. Mean Friday excess returns were 0.013% and -0.081%, respectively.

A high close-location component also puts the stock closer to the breakout boundary: breakout separation is not by itself evidence of general directional or economic skill. Read the stall rates and continuous returns alongside breakout frequencies.
